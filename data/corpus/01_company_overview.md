# Helix Robotics: Company Overview

Helix Robotics Private Limited was founded in Pune in March 2019 by Anjali Deshmukh and Rohan Kulkarni. The company designs autonomous warehouse robots for mid-sized Indian logistics operators. Its headquarters moved from Pune to Whitefield, Bangalore in January 2023, and the Pune site remains the primary manufacturing facility.

As of the 2025 annual report, Helix employs 214 people across engineering, manufacturing, field operations and sales. The engineering team is the largest group with 96 people. The company's fiscal year runs from April to March.

Helix's flagship product line is the Orbit series. The Orbit 2 is a shelf-carrying robot rated for a 600 kg payload, and the Orbit Lite is a smaller unit rated for 250 kg. Both models use the in-house navigation stack called Compass, which runs on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin module and uses a combination of 2D lidar and fisheye cameras.

The company's largest customer is QuickCart Logistics, which operates 11 Helix-equipped fulfilment centres. Helix's stated mission is "moving goods so people don't have to". Revenue for FY2024-25 was 187 crore rupees, up from 121 crore rupees the previous year. The board has five members, chaired by Meera Iyer, a former operations head at a national courier company.
