# Product Roadmap 2026

Helix's 2026 roadmap has three headline items. The first is the Orbit 3, a 1,000 kg payload robot targeted at automotive parts warehouses, scheduled for pilot deployment in August 2026 and general availability in Q1 2027. The Orbit 3 will move from lidar-first to a camera-first perception design using six fisheye cameras and a single safety lidar.

The second item is Compass 4.0, planned for November 2026. Its main features are multi-floor navigation using lift integration, a new learned local planner trained on 1.2 million recorded trajectories, and support for mixed fleets that include third-party robots via the VDA 5050 interface. Compass 4.0 will drop support for Ubuntu 20.04.

The third item is international expansion. Helix opened a sales office in Dubai in April 2026 and plans a first Middle East deployment with a grocery distributor before the end of the year. The company is also in talks with a Vietnamese electronics manufacturer.

Research projects that are not yet committed to the roadmap include a robotic arm attachment for piece picking, codenamed Grasp, and a battery-swap station that would cut charging downtime to under 3 minutes. The roadmap is owned by the chief product officer, Karthik Menon, and is reviewed by the board every quarter. Helix aims to reach 500 deployed robots by December 2026, up from 312 at the end of 2025.
