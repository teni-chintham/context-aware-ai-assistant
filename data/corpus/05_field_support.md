# Field Support and Incident Procedures

Helix runs a 24x7 support desk out of Bangalore reachable at support@helixrobotics.example and on the internal extension 7100. Incidents are classified into four severities. Severity 1 means an entire fleet is stopped and must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and resolved or mitigated within 4 hours. Severity 2 means a single robot is blocking an aisle, with a 30-minute acknowledgement target. Severity 3 covers degraded performance and Severity 4 covers cosmetic or documentation issues.

Each customer site has a named field engineer. Field engineers carry a standard spares kit containing two lidar units, four drive wheels, one battery pack and one Jetson module. Spares are restocked from the Pune warehouse, which ships within one business day.

The most common field fault in 2025 was drive-wheel wear on polished concrete floors, accounting for 31 percent of all tickets. The second most common was Wi-Fi roaming failures at 19 percent. Helix responded to the wheel issue by introducing the Duratread polyurethane wheel in Orbit firmware batch 2025-Q3, which extended average wheel life from 7 months to 14 months.

Preventive maintenance is scheduled every 1,500 operating hours and takes about 45 minutes per robot. A robot that has logged more than 20 unplanned stops in a week is automatically flagged for inspection by Conductor. Customer-facing incident reports must be sent within 2 business days of resolution and are reviewed by the head of field operations, Sameer Joshi.
