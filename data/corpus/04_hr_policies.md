# Helix Robotics Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

Working hours at Helix are 9:30 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday, with a flexible window of one hour on either side. Engineering staff may work remotely up to two days a week; manufacturing and field staff are on-site. Core collaboration hours, when everyone is expected to be reachable, are 11 am to 4 pm.

Employees receive 24 days of paid leave per year, accrued monthly, plus 10 public holidays chosen each January. Up to 8 unused leave days may be carried into the next year. Sick leave is separate and capped at 12 days. New parents receive 26 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks of paternity leave.

Every employee gets a learning budget of 40,000 rupees per year, which can be spent on courses, conferences or books, and requires manager approval above 10,000 rupees per item. The company reimburses one professional certification per year in full.

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Promotions are decided in the April cycle only. The referral bonus for a successful engineering hire is 50,000 rupees, paid after the new hire completes three months. Notice period is 60 days for all roles. The employee assistance helpline is run by a third party and is available 24 hours a day. Laptops are refreshed every three years, and the standard engineering machine is a 16-inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad P1, at the employee's choice.
